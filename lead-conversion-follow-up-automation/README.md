AIMS

When a Lead is converted into an Account and Contact, I want to automate the first steps of the customer handover.

The new Contact receives a welcome email with a link to a PDF I've uploaded to Salesforce Files. The PDF contains useful information about the company. If no Contact email address is found, the email is skipped.

At the same time, Salesforce creates a follow-up Task for the Account Owner, reminding them to get in touch with the new customer within three days.

The idea is to reduce manual work and make sure new customers don't get forgotten after conversion.
